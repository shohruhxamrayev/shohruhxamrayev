Movie Recommendations

Films that stuck with me, worth your time too.

Stranger Things

Created by the Duffer Brothers

A love letter to 80s sci-fi and horror — a small Indiana town, a missing kid, and a growing tangle of government secrets and things that shouldn't exist.

Interstellar

Directed by Christopher Nolan

A dying Earth, a father-daughter bond stretched across galaxies, and one of the most emotionally devastating uses of time dilation in film.

Game of Thrones

Created by David Benioff & D. B. Weiss

Power, politics, and war across the Seven Kingdoms — ruthless in how it treats its characters, and unforgettable because of it.
